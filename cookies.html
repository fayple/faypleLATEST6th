Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Fayple Studios uses cookies and similar browser storage technologies (such as local storage) on our website.

1. What are cookies and local storage?

Cookies and local storage are small pieces of data saved in your browser by a website. They let the site remember things between visits — for example, that you are signed in.

2. What we use

We use only strictly necessary storage. We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies.

  • Sign-in session (local storage) — keeps you signed in after logging in with Discord. Removed when you sign out.
  • Theme preference (local storage) — remembers the colour theme you selected.
  • Cookie notice (local storage) — remembers that you dismissed the cookie notice so it isn't shown again.

3. Third-party cookies

When you sign in, you are sent to Discord, which sets its own cookies under the Discord Privacy Policy. Our hosting and content-delivery providers (Netlify, Google Fonts, jsDelivr) may process technical data needed to deliver files to your browser.

4. Managing storage

You can delete cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings. Because we only use necessary storage, blocking it may stop you from staying signed in or keeping your theme.

5. Changes

If we ever add non-essential cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required by law.

6. Contact

Questions? Reach us through our official Discord server. See also our Privacy Policy.
