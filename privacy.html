Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Fayple Studios ("we", "us") collects when you use our website (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. We aim to collect as little as possible.

1. Information we collect

  • Discord account information. When you sign in with Discord we request only the identify scope. This gives us your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar. We do not receive your email address, password, messages or server list.
  • Session data. A sign-in session token and your selected theme are stored in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. See our Cookie Policy.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider and infrastructure may automatically process standard request data such as IP address, browser type, device information and access times for security and to deliver the website.
  • Content you submit. Administrators may publish announcements, updates, files and collaborator details. Anything published there is visible to signed-in users.

2. How we use information

  • to sign you in and keep your session active;
  • to show your name and avatar in your profile menu;
  • to operate, secure and improve the Service, and prevent abuse;
  • to respond to support requests, legal requests and DMCA notices.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Service providers

We rely on trusted third parties to run the Service. They process data on our behalf or under their own privacy policies:

  • Discord — sign-in (OAuth2).
  • Netlify — website hosting and serverless functions.
  • Supabase — storage of downloadable files.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr — delivery of fonts and scripts, which may see your IP address when your browser loads them.

4. Data retention

Session data stays in your browser until you sign out or clear your browser storage. We keep other information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law.

5. Children's privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users must confirm they are 13 or older before signing in. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. You can sign out at any time, clear your browser storage, and revoke Fayple Studios' access in Discord under User Settings → Authorized Apps. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including HTTPS and limited access permissions. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, they use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us through our official Discord server.
