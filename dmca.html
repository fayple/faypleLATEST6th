DMCA Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Fayple Studios respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users of our website to do the same. We respond to clear notices of alleged copyright infringement in accordance with the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act ("DMCA"), 17 U.S.C. § 512, and similar laws.

1. Reporting infringement

If you believe content available on our website infringes your copyright, send us a written notice that includes:

  1. your physical or electronic signature (or that of a person authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner);
  2. identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed;
  3. identification of the infringing material and enough information to locate it (for example, the file name, announcement or a direct link);
  4. your contact information — name, address, telephone number and email address;
  5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law;
  6. a statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

2. Where to send notices

Send DMCA notices to Fayple Studios through our official Discord server by contacting a staff member, with the subject "DMCA Notice". Incomplete notices may not be processed.

3. What happens next

When we receive a valid notice, we will remove or disable access to the material promptly, and make a reasonable effort to notify the person who posted it.

4. Counter-notification

If you believe material was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice including:

  1. your physical or electronic signature;
  2. identification of the material that was removed and where it appeared before removal;
  3. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification;
  4. your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate court and will accept service of process from the person who filed the original notice.

Unless the original complainant notifies us that they have filed a court action, we may restore the material 10–14 business days after receiving a valid counter-notice.

5. Repeat infringers

We will, in appropriate circumstances, terminate access for users or collaborators who are repeat infringers.

6. Misrepresentation

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), anyone who knowingly makes material misrepresentations in a notice or counter-notice may be liable for damages. Please make sure the content actually infringes your rights before submitting a notice.

7. Roblox content

For content hosted on Roblox itself (such as games or assets on the Roblox platform), please also use Roblox's own reporting tools, as Roblox controls that platform.
